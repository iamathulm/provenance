# Runbook: In-flight battery fault

Owner: Flight Operations. Applies to Talon X2 and Harrier Mini.

## Symptoms
GroundLink shows a red battery warning, a sudden drop of more than 10 percent charge in under a minute, or a cell voltage imbalance alert.

## Immediate actions
Land within 90 seconds at the nearest safe location. Do not continue the mission, even if the remaining charge looks sufficient.
Never hot-swap batteries in flight or with the aircraft powered on. Power the aircraft off before removing any battery.

## After landing
Swap both batteries for fully charged packs before the next flight.
Place the faulty pack in the fireproof battery locker if it is swollen, damaged or hotter than 60 degrees Celsius.
Log the event in GroundLink under Fleet, Battery Health, and attach the flight log.

## Returns
Open a return merchandise authorisation through the support portal for any battery that triggered this runbook. Batteries under warranty are replaced within 5 business days.
