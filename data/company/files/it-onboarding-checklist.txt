# IT onboarding checklist

Owner: IT. Every new employee completes these steps in their first week.

## Day one
Collect your laptop from the IT desk or confirm delivery for remote starts.
Set up 1Password and enrol a second factor for multi-factor authentication.
Join Slack and the announcements, it-help and sec-incidents channels.

## First week
Complete security awareness training in the learning portal.
Request GroundLink sandbox access through the it-help channel if your role works with flight data.
Confirm that full-disk encryption is enabled; IT checks this automatically within 48 hours.

## Getting help
Open a ticket in the it-help channel. The IT desk responds within 4 business hours.
