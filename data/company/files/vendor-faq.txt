# Vendor FAQ

Answers to common questions from suppliers working with Kestrel Ridge Systems.

## How do I get paid?
Invoices are paid net 45 days from receipt and must reference a valid purchase order number.

## How do I update my bank details?
Bank details can only be changed through the procurement portal. Procurement verifies every change by phone before the next payment.
